# JohnDoe GmbH Benefits Guide 2024

A quick-reference summary of all employee benefits.

---

## Health & Wellness

### Medical Insurance
- **JohnDoe covers:** 90% employee, 75% dependents
- **Effective:** First of month after start date
- **Open enrollment:** November 1–15 each year
- **Plans:** PPO Gold, PPO Silver, HDHP+HSA

### Mental Health
- EAP: 8 free therapy sessions/year via BetterHelp integration
- Mental health days: no documentation required (same as sick days)
- Calm and Headspace subscriptions: company-paid

### Fitness
- **$50/month gym reimbursement** — submit receipts via HR portal
- On-site fitness center at HQ (free for all employees)
- Ergonomic equipment reimbursement: up to $300 every 2 years

---

## Financial Benefits

### 401(k)
- **Match:** 100% up to 4% of salary
- **Vesting:** 4-year cliff (you own 0% until year 4, then 100%)
- **Enrollment:** After 3 months; automatic enrollment at 3% if no action taken
- **Provider:** Fidelity — manage at netbenefits.fidelity.com

### Equity (RSUs)
- Eligible: All full-time employees with rating 3+
- Standard grant: vests over 4 years (25%/year) with 1-year cliff
- Refresher grants: reviewed at annual performance cycle for top performers

### Referral Bonus
- **$2,500** for successful full-time hire referrals
- Paid 6 months after referred employee's start date
- No limit on referrals per employee per year

## Professional Development

### Annual L&D Budget: $2,000
Approved uses:
- Online courses (Coursera, Pluralsight, O'Reilly, etc.)
- Industry conferences (registration + travel)
- Professional certifications (exam fees + prep materials)
- Technical books

### Tuition Reimbursement: $5,250/year
- For job-related undergraduate or graduate programs
- Requires manager + HR approval before enrollment
- 2-year stay requirement after program completion
- Grade of B or better required

## Additional Perks

- **Commuter benefits:** Up to $315/month pre-tax for transit/parking
- **Home office stipend:** $500 one-time for remote-primary employees
- **Company laptop:** MacBook Pro or Windows equivalent, refreshed every 3 years
- **Team offsites:** Annual all-hands + quarterly team events, expenses covered
- **Employee discounts:** 20% off JohnDoe products; partnerships with retail/travel vendors
