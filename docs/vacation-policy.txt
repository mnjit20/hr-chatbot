JohnDoe GmbH — VACATION AND TIME-OFF POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: October 2024

=============================================================
1. VACATION ENTITLEMENT
=============================================================

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 paid vacation days per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive vacation days on a pro-rated basis according to their
scheduled hours relative to a full-time 40-hour week.

Vacation days are accrued at a rate of 1.67 days per month, starting from the
first day of employment. Newly hired employees may use accrued vacation after
completing their 90-day probationary period.

=============================================================
2. CARRYOVER POLICY
=============================================================

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the following
calendar year. These carry-over days must be used by March 31st of the new year
or they will be forfeited. There is no cash payout for forfeited carry-over days.

=============================================================
3. REQUESTING VACATION
=============================================================

Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least:
- 2 weeks in advance for absences of 1-3 days
- 4 weeks in advance for absences of 4-9 days
- 8 weeks in advance for absences of 10+ consecutive days

Requests are subject to manager approval and business needs. Approval is not
guaranteed during peak periods such as fiscal quarter-end, product launch windows,
or other business-critical periods identified by department heads.

=============================================================
4. VACATION PAY
=============================================================

Vacation pay is calculated at the employee's regular base rate of pay. Vacation
time does not count toward overtime calculations. Vacation pay is not included
in bonus calculations.

=============================================================
5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
=============================================================

JohnDoe GmbH observes 10 federal public holidays per year. These are not deducted
from vacation entitlement. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the closest
weekday will be observed as the holiday.

=============================================================
6. UNUSED VACATION ON SEPARATION
=============================================================

Upon resignation or termination, employees will be paid out for accrued but unused
vacation days up to a maximum of 20 days. This payout is calculated at the
employee's current base rate of pay.

For questions about your vacation balance, please contact HR at hr@johndoegmbh.com
or use the HR portal self-service feature.
