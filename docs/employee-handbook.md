# John Doe GmbH Employee Handbook

**Version 4.2 | Updated: Q4 2024**

---

## Welcome to John Doe GmbH

We're glad you're here. This handbook outlines the policies, benefits, and expectations that govern your employment at John Doe GmbH. Please read it carefully and reach out to HR with any questions.

---

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, partners, and customers with respect
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary and customer information
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts to your manager
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies
- Report suspected misconduct to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## Working Hours

**Core hours:** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in your local timezone (all employees must be available during this window).

**Flexible hours:** Outside core hours, you may structure your 8-hour workday as best suits your productivity, subject to manager agreement.

**Remote work:** Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Full remote arrangements (4–5 days) require VP-level approval and are reviewed annually. All remote employees must maintain a reliable internet connection and a professional work environment for video calls.

---

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted **twice per year**:

- **Mid-year review:** July (calibration in late June)
- **Annual review:** January (calibration in late December)

**Rating scale:**
| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | Significantly exceeds all expectations |
| 4 – Exceeds | Consistently exceeds expectations |
| 3 – Meets | Fully meets expectations |
| 2 – Developing | Partially meets expectations |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | Does not meet expectations |

Employees with a rating of **4 or above** are eligible for merit-based salary increases. Ratings of **2 or below** trigger a 90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

## Benefits Overview

### Health Insurance

John Doe GmbH covers **90% of health insurance premiums** for employees and **75% for dependents**. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date.

Available plans:
- PPO Gold (low deductible, higher premium)
- PPO Silver (balanced)
- HDHP with HSA (high deductible, Acme contributes $500/year to HSA)

### Dental & Vision

- Dental: 100% coverage for preventive care; 80% for basic restorative
- Vision: $200 annual allowance for frames/contacts; eye exams covered 100%

### 401(k) Retirement Plan

Acme matches **100% of contributions up to 4% of your salary**. Matching contributions vest on a 4-year cliff schedule. Employees are eligible to enroll after 3 months of employment.

### Life Insurance

Company-paid life insurance at **2x your annual base salary** up to $500,000. Additional coverage may be purchased at your own expense.

### Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Free, confidential counseling (up to 8 sessions per year), financial planning, and legal consultation services. Available to all employees and their household members. Contact: 1-800-EAP-HELP (available 24/7).

## Learning & Development

- **Annual L&D budget:** $2,000 per employee for courses, conferences, certifications, and books
- **Tuition reimbursement:** Up to $5,250/year for job-related degree programs (requires 2-year commitment post-completion)
- **Internal training:** Monthly lunch-and-learns, quarterly engineering talks, and an internal mentorship program

---

## Equipment & Technology

New employees receive a laptop (MacBook Pro 14" or Windows equivalent) and any peripherals needed for their role. A **$500 home office stipend** is provided to remote-primary employees upon joining.

Software licenses are managed by IT. Submit software requests via the IT portal. Personal use of company equipment for minor tasks is permitted but use for any illegal, unethical, or competing activities is strictly prohibited.

---

## Expense Policy

- Meals while traveling: **up to $75/day**
- Hotel: **up to $250/night** in most cities; pre-approval required for higher-cost cities
- Air travel: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; business class pre-approval required for longer flights
- All expenses over **$25 require a receipt**
- Submit expense reports within **30 days** of incurring the expense

## Parental Leave

| Leave Type | Duration | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Maternity Leave | 16 weeks | 100% base pay |
| Paternity / Partner Leave | 8 weeks | 100% base pay |
| Adoption Leave | 16 weeks | 100% base pay |

Leave may be taken continuously or intermittently (with HR approval) within 12 months of the child's birth or placement. Notify HR at least 30 days in advance when possible.

---

## Sick Leave

John Doe GmbH offers **unlimited sick leave** for personal illness. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than **5 consecutive business days**. Sick leave cannot be used for non-illness reasons (use vacation or personal days instead).

Mental health days are treated the same as physical illness days — no documentation required for single-day absences.
